Solo recital · Barcelona · 11 April

Casa de Rusia

Four composers. One dramatic arc.

Casa de Rusia presented Tinatin in collaboration with the Conservatori Liceu for a 90-minute Thursday evening recital in Barcelona.

VenueCasa de Rusia, Barcelona
Time20:00–21:30
PartnerConservatori Liceu
FormatSolo recital

Program

  • Robert Schumann — Fantasiestücke, Op. 12
  • Enrique Granados — Goyescas: “El amor y la muerte”
  • Frédéric Chopin — Scherzo No. 1
  • Sergei Rachmaninoff — Étude-Tableau, Op. 39 No. 6

What students can learn from it

A recital program is more than a list of pieces. It is pacing, contrast, stamina, emotional architecture, and the confidence to carry an audience from one musical world into another.
