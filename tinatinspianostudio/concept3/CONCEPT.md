# Concept 3 — A Modern Musical Life

## Position

Fresh, editorial, optimistic.

The site presents piano study as a rewarding part of contemporary life—not an old-fashioned obligation. It should appeal equally to parents, adult learners, and ambitious students.

## Core idea

**Fall in love with what you can play.**

This concept is the most conversion-forward and content-friendly. It combines clean editorial typography with lively student photography and a modular system Tinatin can maintain.

## Visual direction

- Clean warm white.
- Ink-black typography.
- Muted cobalt or ultramarine for primary actions.
- Apricot or saffron highlight used in small blocks.
- Sage-gray secondary background.
- Expressive high-contrast serif display type.
- Clear geometric sans-serif for navigation and body copy.
- Modular photo collage inspired by an editorial magazine.
- Crisp cards for audiences, testimonials, and resources.

## Homepage hero

Eyebrow: Private Piano Lessons in Clearwater and Online

Headline:

**Fall in love with what you can play.**

Supporting copy:

Personal piano lessons for curious beginners, returning adults, and advanced musicians—built around your goals, pace, and musical voice.

Primary action: Book a Free Trial Lesson

Secondary action: Find Your Lesson Path

## Homepage flow

1. Split hero with strong headline and teaching-photo collage.
2. Audience cards: Children, Adults, Advanced.
3. “What progress looks like here.”
4. Tinatin’s teaching method.
5. Credentials and performance proof.
6. Student stories.
7. Featured piano tip or resource.
8. Pricing and lesson options.
9. FAQ.
10. Trial booking.

## Content system

Two reusable formats:

### Performance story

Lead image, program, venue, video, reflection, and teaching connection.

### Piano note

Short practical article or video covering technique, practice, interpretation, or motivation.

Both formats should appear as editorial cards on the homepage and have clean archive pages.

## Photography brief

- Mixed-age students in genuine lesson moments.
- Tinatin interacting rather than looking at the camera.
- Detail photographs of hands, scores, pencil notes, and the piano mechanism.
- One confident color portrait of Tinatin.
- Crops designed for overlapping editorial collage layouts.

## Reference influence

Primary: Payam Music

Supporting: Studio Ronde and Víkingur Ólafsson

## Best fit

The most contemporary and marketable direction, especially if the website will support regular articles, short videos, social content, and seasonal enrollment campaigns.
