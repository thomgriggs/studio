# Concept 1 — The Private Studio

## Position

Warm, cultivated, personal.

Tinatin is presented as the trusted teacher who brings serious musical training into an encouraging private-studio relationship.

## Core idea

**A lifelong relationship with music begins here.**

The emotional center is the student’s growth: confidence, fluency, discipline, and enjoyment. Tinatin’s performance credentials support that promise without dominating it.

## Visual direction

- Warm ivory background.
- Deep forest green for navigation, text, and major actions.
- Burgundy or oxblood as a limited expressive accent.
- Soft brass only for fine rules or small details.
- Large contemporary sans-serif headings.
- Elegant italic serif used sparingly for emotional phrases.
- Natural-light photography with cream walls, wood, black lacquer, and quiet studio details.
- Rounded image corners and generous breathing room.
