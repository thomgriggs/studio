About Me

I AM TINATIN GUDADZE

Welcome to my Piano Studio

I am a Georgian/Spanish pianist. I started playing piano at the age of five, and since then piano has become one of the pillars of my life.

Teaching piano, I have discovered three main points that make students succeed: motivation, time efficiency, and practical details. Being a performer pianist enables me to set realistic goals for each student and motivate them to achieve them.
