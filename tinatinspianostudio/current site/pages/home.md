## About Tinatin

### GET TO KNOW ME BETTER!

### I AM TINATIN GUDADZE

I am a Georgian/Spanish pianist and I would like to give you a warm welcome to my Piano Studio. I started playing piano at the age of 5 and since then, piano has become one of the pillars of my life.

Teaching piano I have discovered that there are three main key points that make students succeed: Motivation, Time Efficiency and Practice Details. Being a performer pianist enables me to set realistic goals for each student and motivate them to achieve it.

Technical challenges? Sight reading difficulties? Improvement stucked? You aren’t alone, I know how to help.
