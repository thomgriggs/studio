# Concept 2 — From Stage to Studio

## Position

Dramatic, accomplished, aspirational.

Tinatin’s life as a concert pianist becomes the differentiator: students learn from someone who understands preparation, interpretation, confidence, and performance from lived experience.

## Core idea

**The discipline of the stage. The attention of a private lesson.**

This concept gives Tinatin the greatest artistic authority while maintaining a clear lesson funnel.

## Visual direction

- Charcoal and near-black foundation.
- Warm parchment content panels.
- Muted champagne-gold accent.
- Occasional deep wine tone.
- Editorial serif headlines with restrained uppercase sans-serif labels.
- Cinematic black-and-white or low-saturation performance photography.
- Thin rules, large margins, and deliberate asymmetry.
- Subtle motion inspired by a curtain opening or a page turn.

## Homepage hero

Eyebrow: Classical Piano Instruction · Children · Adults · Advanced Study

Headline:

**Bring greater depth to every note.**

Supporting copy:

Study with concert pianist Tinatin Gudadze in a private studio devoted to technique, interpretation, and meaningful musical progress.

Primary action: Book a Free Trial Lesson

Secondary action: Meet Tinatin

## Homepage flow

1. Full-screen performance or studio portrait.
2. Immediate lesson-path selector.
3. Tinatin’s performer-to-teacher philosophy.
4. Credentials and selected venues.
5. Featured performance story.
6. Student outcomes and testimonials.
7. Lesson formats and pricing.
8. Trial-lesson process.
9. Final stage-inspired call to action.

## Performance system

Each featured performance should include:

- Program title.
- Date and venue.
- Repertoire.
- One lead image.
- Video.
- Short background story.
- Tinatin’s reflection.
- A teaching insight connected to the performance.

## Photography brief

- Tinatin on stage at a grand piano.
- Close portrait in concert attire.
- Rehearsal images with dramatic side light.
- Empty keyboard and bench before a performance.
- Teaching image with a more intimate, warmer grade to soften the concept.

## Reference influence

Primary: Hélène Grimaud

Supporting: Víkingur Ólafsson and Virtu.Academy

## Best fit

The strongest direction if Tinatin wants advanced students, audition preparation, and performance study to carry significant weight in the business.

## Primary risk

The design must repeatedly signal warmth and accessibility so beginners and parents do not assume the studio is only for elite pianists.
