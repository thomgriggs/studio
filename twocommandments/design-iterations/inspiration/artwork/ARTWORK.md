# Artwork — public-domain references for the two commandments

Pulled 2026-10-05 from the Art Institute of Chicago open-access API (CC0) and Wikimedia Commons (works marked Public domain). Everything here may be used freely; credit lines below. These are *references and stand-ins* for the “Jesus in the background” panels, the Good Samaritan (Luke 10), the Law and the Prophets, and the four Gospels — not the founder’s stock/AI links, which need licensing. Images are saved at preview size; the source links give full resolution.
