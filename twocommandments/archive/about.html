Panel 18

It's all about love, and taking care of people.

II Commandments is a movement first and a store second. The message is simple enough to fit on a shirt; the shirt is a way to carry it, and a way to take care of the people who make it and the people who need it.

Where every dollar goes

    One third to the church or non-profit you choose. One third to the designer who made it. One third keeps II Commandments running — marketing, printing, shipping, and answering your questions. One percent to a foundation (to be named).

    For designers

    Graphic design work is being farmed out to machines. This is a place where a designer can make something real, tell their story, and earn from it — with the front of house (marketing, drumming up sales) and the back of house (printing, shipping, support) handled for them.
