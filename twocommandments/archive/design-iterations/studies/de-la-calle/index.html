Four rooms

What's borrowed: the ticker bar; the boxed nav with the brand in the middle; a hero whose whole background is the product's colour, with the product as a tilted can-card and a drip badge; condensed caps everywhere; a best-sellers row of cards each in its own colour — and the room-to-room feeling: tap a card and the entire hero changes colour and content. The rooms here are the Gospels; for the store they'd be designers.
