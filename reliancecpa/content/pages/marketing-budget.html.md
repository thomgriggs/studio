[h3] A CPA in Tampa Discusses the Marketing Budget for Your Small Business
[img] alt="Tampa CPA" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/marketing-budget.gif
[p] Marketing is defined, simply, as a way to get potential consumers interested in what you’re selling. From that simple definition has arisen an entire industry of marketing professionals whose purpose is to help businesses get the word out about their products and services. It can be an expensive process, made even more expensive if you choose to operate without an extremely focused perspective. Scatter-shot marketing might work for some, but chances are, if you choose that path, your message will fall on deaf ears – and the effort will have gone for nothing.
[p] A [CPA in Tampa](file:///S:/websites/Reliance/index.html) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help you find the focus you need to conduct a well-thought-out, efficient, affordable marketing campaign. It starts with you, the business owner, who has the ability to provide a unique product or service to the public. It ends with you making contact with that customer base, whose willingness to purchase what you’re selling will, of course, make or break your business. But how do you get from Point A to Point B? One of the first steps is to decide how much money you can afford to spend on marketing, followed closely by a decision about how best to spend that money.
[p] The process of developing a marketing budget can be broken down into three main parts, all of which carry their own unique costs:
[ul]
- Design or create a product or service that will be desirable for potential customers.
- Research how best to package the product or service, including pricing.
- Promote the product or service using public relations, advertising, marketing communications, sales, and distribution.
