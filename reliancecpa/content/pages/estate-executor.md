[h3] A CPA in Tampa Talks About the Choice of Executor of Your Estate
[img] alt="Estate Executor" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/estate-executor-300x199.jpg
[p] Perhaps the most important decision you can make in the course of estate planning is who to name as executor. While it may seem logical to simply name your primary heir as executor – and many people do – this is not always the most prudent path. For one thing, a close family member or friend who stands to inherit money or property from the estate may experience emotional trauma upon your passing, and the key quality for any executor is level-headedness. In addition to maintaining an even keel during a potentially emotional process, an executor must be organized and have a working knowledge of the legalities surrounding inheritance and probate.
