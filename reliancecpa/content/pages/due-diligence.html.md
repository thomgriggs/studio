[h3] A Tampa CPA Discusses Due Diligence When Starting A Small Business
[img] alt="Tampa CPA" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/due-diligence.gif
[p] The term “due diligence” can have a legal connotation, specifically when it applies to research and investigations performed prior to signing a contract. However, the term more commonly applies to voluntary investigations, such as when an entrepreneur investigates a business in the course of acquiring its assets. It can also apply to someone researching all the legal and practical details of starting a new business from scratch.
