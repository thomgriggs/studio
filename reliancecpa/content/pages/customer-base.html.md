[h3] A CPA in Tampa on the Importance of Analyzing the Customer Base for a New Business
[img] alt="CPA Tampa" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/customer-base.gif
[p] Perhaps the most important element of planning a new business is learning as much as possible about the people you hope will purchase the product or service you plan to offer. Quite likely, you decided to start the business to meet a perceived need in your chosen market, so you probably already have an idea about the identity of your potential clients. That’s a good start, but your early notion of your customers’ needs is sure to evolve with research, thereby strengthening your ability to meet their needs.
[p] A [CPA in Tampa](http://www.reliancecpa.com/) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help you develop a research plan that will eliminate uncertainty about your customer base. The information you need to gain a thorough understanding of your potential clients includes:
[ul]
- Where they live
- Age
- Gender
- Ethnic background
- Education level
- Income level
- Marital status
