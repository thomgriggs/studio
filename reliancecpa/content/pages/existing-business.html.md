[h3] A Tampa CPA Compares Buying an Existing Business to Starting a New Business
[img] alt="CPA Tampa" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/existing-business.gif
[p] If you find yourself wanting to enter the business world as an owner, you basically have two choices: start your own from scratch, or purchase an existing company. Each course of action has its benefits and potential drawbacks, and ultimately, the decision will be based on personal preference. However, until you know more, how will you decide which path you prefer?

[p] Here is a brief comparison of starting a business and buying an existing company:
[ul]
- Buying an existing business eliminates startup costs; starting a business allows you to determine the original vision and initial goals, rather than trying to adjust to someone else’s ideas.
- Starting a business means you are likely to have to establish cash flow to begin; an existing business can be presumed to have cash flow in place.
- Buying a business means you inherit the potential problems (customer relations, legal, etc.) of the former owner; starting your own business allows you to begin with a clean slate and establish relationships on your terms.
