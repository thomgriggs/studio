[h3] A Tampa CPA Discusses How E-Commerce Can Help Grow a Small Business
[img] alt="CPA Tampa" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/small-business-ecommerce.gif
[p] Not every business can thrive based solely on a strong presence on the Internet. For example, an independent fisherman would be hard-pressed to find a way to make a website help him catch more fish every day. However, that same fisherman might be able to use a website to help build a customer base, as well as better serve the customers he already has. And some businesses – such as a book distributor or an apparel designer – can do very well with the right business plan combined with a modicum of Internet savvy.
[p] A [Tampa CPA](http://www.reliancecpa.com//index.html) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help you get the most out of your company’s Internet presence. Since opening our doors in 1984, Reliance has helped hundreds of small businesses reach their full potential, and we can do the same for a business that relies heavily on e-commerce. The benefits of e-commerce include:
[ul]
- Lower business costs – no “brick-and-mortar” business headquarters required, just a laptop and (if applicable) a central warehouse.
- Easy access – customers can shop for your service or products 24 hours a day, seven days a week.
- Streamlined customer service – requiring a log-in function allows instant recognition of return customers, who can receive personalized recommendations.
[p] This is only a small sample of how a small business can benefit from the proper use of e-commerce.
