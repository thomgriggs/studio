[h3] A Tampa Dental CPA Provides Practice Acquisition Analysis
[img] alt="Dental Practice Acquisition Analysis" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/Dental-Practice-Acquisition-Analysis-300x200.jpg
[p] Buying a dental practice is one of the most momentous career decisions a dentist can make. Before the purchase can be made, though, it requires thorough, professional practice acquisition analysis by an experienced dental CPA. The certified professional accountants at Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help you with everything required to make an informed decision when you want to buy a dental practice.
[p] The first piece of information you’ll want to find out as you and your [dental CPA](http://reliancecpa.com/dental-cpa) conduct a dental practice acquisition analysis is why the practice is being sold. The answer to this question might go a long way toward convincing you that this is the kind of practice you want to buy into – or warn you off the purchase early enough to avoid a lot of wasted time. If you are satisfied with the reason the practice is being sold, there are many other details to find out. These include:
[ul]
- The number of active patients the practice has maintained during the past year
- Number of patients with insurance
- Demographics of the patient pool
- New patients over the past month
- Revenue generated each month by hygiene
- Hygiene schedule in recent weeks
- Fee schedule
- Average insurance adjustment fees
- Average office hours worked by doctors and staff
- And more
