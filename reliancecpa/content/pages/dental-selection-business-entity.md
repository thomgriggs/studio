[ul]
- [Limited liability corporation](http://www.reliancecpa.com/dental-llp)
- [S corporation](http://www.reliancecpa.com/dental-s-corporation)
- Partnership
- [C corporation](http://www.reliancecpa.com/dental-c-corporation)
- Sole proprietorship
[p] The way these entities are structured can vary greatly from company to company. That’s why the selection of your practice’s business entity is never a one-size-fits-all decision.
