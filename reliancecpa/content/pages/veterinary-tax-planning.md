[h2] The Importance of Tax Planning for Small Business Owners
[p] Although tax planning can benefit anyone, it is essential if you own a veterinary practice. Along with standard income tax, you will have to contend with several other forms of taxation. These could include self-employment tax, employment taxes, sales tax and personal property tax. The exact rules governing taxes in each of these areas will largely depend on the business entity you have chosen for your practice.
[h2] Reducing Small Business Taxes
[p] As a small business owner, there are a number of ways that you can limit the amount you pay to the government each year. The accountants at Reliance Consulting will help you identify these areas, so you can keep more money in your veterinary practice. Tax reduction strategies could include:
[ul]
- Incorporation or forming an LLC
- Purchasing property, rather than leasing
- Taking a depreciation allowance for certain equipment
- Developing an advertising campaign that will be tax deductible
- Increasing salary for employees who will boost productivity (Salary is tax deductible.)
- Setting up retirement plans for yourself and your team
- Using accountable plans for employee reimbursements
[p] Not all of these strategies will work for all businesses. Our accountants can evaluate the needs of your practice to determine whether these or other tactics may work for you.
