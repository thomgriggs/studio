Developing empathy with the people who rely on your company’s services or products will help your employees understand the importance of their jobs, as well as the importance of upholding the overall vision of the company.

[ul]
- Make sure your employees understand what clients expect from the company
- Make sure you hire employees with a customer-service mindset
- Make sure employees don’t waste the time of your customers
