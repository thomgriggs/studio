[h3] Your Tampa CPA Explains how a Money Market Account Works
[img] ALT MISSING src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/money-market.gif
[p] In a volatile financial world, many investors turn from the stock market to a money market account as a more secure method of safeguarding investments. While several varieties of money market accounts are available, in general they can be defined as liquid assets based on debt securities that mature in less than a year. Because of the relatively rapid maturation rate, the return on money market investments usually is lower than other securities.
[p] If you seek a safer, less-volatile way to invest your money, a [Tampa CPA](file:///S:/websites/index.html) at Reliance Consulting, LLC, will help you explore your money market options. The debt securities associated with the money market are, in essence, short-term IOUs issued by governments, large corporations or financial institutions. The purpose of these IOUs is to help these well-established institutions raise large amounts of cash quickly.
[p] An accountant from Reliance will analyze your financial circumstance in order to help you determine if any of the following money market instruments are right for you:
[ul]
- Money market mutual funds or bank accounts
- Treasury bills
- Certificates of Deposit (CD)
- Commercial paper
- Banker’s acceptance
- Eurodollars
- Repurchase agreements (repos)
