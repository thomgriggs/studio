[h3] Your Tampa CPA Explains Adjusted Gross Income
[img] alt="Tampa CPA" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/adjusted-gross-income.gif
[p] Adjusted gross income is the adjusted dollar amount used by the IRS to determine how much income tax you must pay. The gross amount of money you received through salary, wages and other forms of income constitute your gross income. Most people are eligible to make deductions and other adjustments to their gross income, producing the bottom-line taxable figure. This is not to be confused with tax credits, which are figured in after your income tax is determined. Sound complicated? Not to worry.

- Business expenses, including rental activity

- Some moving expenses
