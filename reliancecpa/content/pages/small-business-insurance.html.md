Make no mistake, though, an improperly insured business is vulnerable to all sorts of loss – including embezzlement, lawsuits, a natural disaster and more. How is a business owner to know what kinds of policies to purchase?

That includes providing guidance for which types of insurance to buy, which can include:

- Workers’ compensation
