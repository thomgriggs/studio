[img] alt="Tampa CPA" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/Bottom-Line-300x200.jpg
[p] Most people in business are all-too-familiar with the term “bottom line.” It is often used to describe a company’s profit, although it may also be used in general conversation to describe the ultimate result or outcome of an event, or as the deciding or critical factor in an argument. In accounting, the term came into common usage because the final, or bottom, line of a financial statement usually is reserved to display net income or net loss.
