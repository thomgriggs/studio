[h3] What to Ask a CPA in Tampa Before Buying a Home
[img] alt="Tampa CPA Buying Home" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/buying-home.gif
[p] For most of us, buying a home is the largest investment of our lives. It should never be undertaken lightly, and thorough research is absolutely necessary before making a commitment to buy a house, townhome, or condominium unit. There are dozens of factors to consider, including the amount of living space needed for the family, the quality of nearby schools, the congeniality of the neighborhood, and much more. Aside from personal preferences, though, the most important factor to consider is whether you can actually afford to buy the home you have your eye on.
[p] A [CPA in Tampa](http://www.reliancecpa.com/) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can be a valuable partner as you contemplate buying a new home. While you may have some notion of the short-term and long-term costs involved with purchasing property, a certified public accountant from Reliance can help you analyze, down to the finest detail, your ability to comfortably afford the home of your choice. The questions to ask a Reliance CPA include:
[ul]
- What types of loans are available, and which best fits my financial situation?
- Based on my long-term financial situation, how much should my down payment be?
- How much should my monthly mortgage payment be in order to fit my budget?
- What financial documents and records do I need to secure a loan?
- What tax issues should I take into consideration?
- Does it make more financial sense for me to rent or buy a home?
[p] There is much more to buying a home than just the financial side, of course. There is an emotional element, as well.
