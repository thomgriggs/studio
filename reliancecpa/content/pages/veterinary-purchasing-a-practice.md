[h2] Buying vs. Starting Your Own Practice
[p] If you want to go into business for yourself, you could purchase an existing practice, or you could open up a brand new clinic. Both options have their pros and cons. The right choice for you will largely depend on your financial resources, personality, and professional goals. The advantages of purchasing an existing practice include:
[ul]
- You will not need to invest in equipment or the various items needed to run a clinic. At the same time, however, you may have higher upfront costs.
- It may be easier to obtain loan approval.
- You will already have an existing clientele, as well as regular suppliers and service providers.
[p] However, when you purchase an older practice, you will be somewhat bound by the existing structure of the clinic. You will also have to work with someone else’s team, rather than handpicked employees.

[h2] Buying into a Franchise
[p] Franchises are less common in veterinary medicine than in other industries. Nevertheless, purchasing a franchise can still be a great option, especially if you are a first-time business owner. Franchises give you instant name recognition. In some cases, you may also receive financial assistance with the purchase. However, you may have to pay ongoing fees, including advertising fees and royalty payments. In addition, you will not have the same freedom that you would have if you owned a non-franchised practice.
[p] When purchasing a franchise, you should do the same research listed above. Examine the financial stability of the company. Consider their history, client satisfaction levels, and rankings with the Better Business Bureau. At Reliance Consulting, we can also help you with these investigations.
