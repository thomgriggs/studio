[h3] A CPA in Tampa Talks About the Importance of a Social Media Strategy for Small Businesses
[img] alt="Social Media Strategy" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/Social-Media-Strategy-291x300.jpg
[p] Any business that ignores social media is foolish to do so. Social sites such as Twitter, Facebook, Pinterest, Instagram, Reddit, Google+, and StumbleUpon might seem like frivolous wastes of time for those caught up in the day-to-day business of running a company. This is not just kid stuff, though. A company’s online presence has never had a greater impact on the bottom line. Consumers not only appreciate a vibrant, engaging online presence – they expect it from the companies they patronize.
[p] A [CPA in Tampa](http://reliancecpa.com) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help you determine how social media fits into your overall marketing strategy. Keep in mind that as social media evolves, the number of consumers from every demographic category continues to increase. Consumers now expect to be able to communicate directly and instantly with the companies they buy from, and that communication often takes place on Twitter or Facebook. Think of it this way. Customer service is obviously an important part of any business. Some companies even hire whole departments just to handle customer service. Well, today’s customer service takes place increasingly online. It’s not merely a luxury – social media marketing is now a part of the cost of doing business.
