[h3] Ask your CPA in Tampa, FL about the Benefits of Becoming an S Corporation
[img] alt="Ask your CPA in Tampa, FL about the Benefits of Becoming an S Corporation" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/s-corporation.jpg
[p] Many companies have begun to explore the potential tax benefits of becoming an S Corporation. Most corporations are C corporations, which means both the corporate profit and the shareholders’ dividends are taxed. In an S corporation, such “double taxation” is avoided, because the company’s income or losses are divided among the shareholders, who report the income or loss on their personal tax returns.
[p] If you are considering incorporation for your new or expanding business, your [CPA in Tampa](http://www.reliancecpa.com/) at Reliance Consulting, LLC can help you determine whether you are eligible to file as S Corporation.
[p] According to the IRS, in order to be qualified to become an S corporation, the following criteria must apply:
[ul]
- The company must be domestic.
- The company’s shareholders may not include partnerships, corporations or non-resident aliens.
- The company may have no more than 100 shareholders.
- The company must have one class of stock.
[p] The name is derived from the fact that companies that make a valid election to become “S” corporations will be taxed under Subchapter S, Chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue Code. Owners of an S corporation enjoy many of the same taxation benefits as legal partnerships and also receive limited liability protection from creditors.
