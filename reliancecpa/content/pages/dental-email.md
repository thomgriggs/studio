[p] Why is an updated [email list](http://www.reliancecpa.com/dental-internet-marketing) so important for a dental practice? There are many reasons, including:
[ul]
- Reminding a patient about an upcoming appointment
- Sending a patient or large group of patients a specific marketing message geared toward shared interests
- Making special discount offers to loyal patients
- Asking for patient feedback on services
- Reminding a patient about payments due
- Inviting a patient to follow, join, or like your dental practice’s social media pages (Twitter, Facebook, etc.)
[p] Every dental software management system has many of these tools integrated within their platforms. Configuring the tools and effectively using them is however the key. Hiring the trainers from the software company is a great strategy that most dentists don’t employ because of higher costs of training. However, this investment is necessary to ensure a robust customer relationship management system. There are many outside tools like Demand Force and Constant Contact that have been successfully implemented within many dental practices.
[p] It’s important to have a plan for using the email addresses you gather from your patients. But your patient data gathering also needs to go beyond just an email address. An appropriate customer relationship management system must be implemented to ensure optimal patient engagement.
