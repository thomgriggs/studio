[p] Choosing the right marketing and advertising agency for your dental practice will go a long way toward determining the practice’s long-term business success. At first, the decision will undoubtedly be based primarily on financial concerns. After all, a small dental practice with only one office and a handful of employees cannot be expected to spend thousands of dollars a month on marketing and advertising. Nor should it spend that much – at first. In general, a small initial investment in getting the word out in your community should be enough to get your practice up and running. After that, though, the competitive nature of the dental industry means it is vital to find a marketing and/or advertising agency you can trust to help you find your rightful place in the dental landscape.
