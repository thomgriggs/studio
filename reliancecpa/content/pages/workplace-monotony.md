[h3] A Tampa CPA Gives Tips on How to Combat Workplace Monotony
[img] alt="Employee Monotony" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/Employee-Monotony-300x200.jpg
[p] If you own a small business and have noticed a decided lack of enthusiasm lately among your employees, it might be time to shake things up. Workplace monotony can poison an environment, lower morale, and reduce production. Yes, there’s a reason it’s called “work,” not “play,” but that doesn’t mean it’s OK to allow the work day to be a miserable experience.
[p] A [Tampa CPA](http://reliancecpa.com) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can provide advice on worker productivity, which is directly influenced by worker morale. As any successful small business owner can tell you, happy workers are productive workers. The pay scale is high on the list of priorities for employees, of course, but studies have found that pay is only slightly more important to workers in most industries than workplace satisfaction. Here are a few ways to make the office more interesting and engaging:
[ul]
- Start an incentive program, such as employee of the month, and attach real value to the reward
- Allow flexible hours, especially for parents of school-age children
- Demonstrate as often as possible that you appreciate the value an individual employee adds to the company
- Surprise your employees occasionally by paying for lunch or an afternoon coffee run
[p] How you transform your office into a place employees want to be each day will depend on your industry, your personality, and the daily demands of your company.
