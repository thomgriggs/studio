[h3] A CPA in Tampa Helps Keep Your Small Business on the Right Track
[img] alt="CPA in Tampa" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/small-business-planning.gif
[p] Any business owner knows how much hard work it takes to get a company off the ground and keep it running at peak efficiency. Once you have that business on the right track, though, how do you keep it there?
[p] Your [CPA in Tampa](http://www.reliancecpa.com/) at Reliance Consulting, LLC, will show you how to build on what you’ve started – and how to make sure your company never veers off course as you attempt to meet your financial goals. How can that be accomplished? It starts with planning, and good business planning involves sound forecasting on your part. Forecasting doesn’t mean you must tap into your inner psychic – no one expects you to know exactly how your cash flow and profit-loss statements will read a year from now. However, the practice of estimating these important factors well in advance is a vital part of understanding how well your business is doing during a given period of time.
[p] The U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) advises forecasting for growth as a means of maintaining the momentum of your small business. The five criteria for strategic thinking offered by the SBA are:
[ul]
- Organization – the way people, resources and business structure are integrated
- Observation – important for understanding motivation, problem solving and recognizing an alternative course of action
- Viewpoints – consider the environment, the marketplace, the individual project and measurements such as cost and potential gain to take appropriate action
- Driving forces – organizational incentives, vision, mission, values or goals
- Ideal position – conditions necessary for the business to be a success
