[h3] CPA in Tampa, FL Discusses Why Your Business Needs an Employer ID Number (EIN)
[img] alt="CPA in Tampa, FL Discusses Why Your Business Needs an Employer ID Number (EIN)" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/employer-identification-number.jpg
[p] A federal employer identification number (EIN), also known as a tax ID number or simply a tax number, is a nine-digit code used by a business to identify itself when filing taxes, banking or conducting other financial transactions like payroll.
[p] Near the top of your to-do list when starting a new business in the Tampa Bay area, or making changes to an existing business, is applying for an employer identification number. Your [CPA in Tampa](http://www.reliancecpa.com/), Reliance Consulting, LLC, can guide you through the sometimes-confounding red tape that comes with starting or operating a business – such as determining whether you need a new EIN, based on Internal Revenue Service guidelines.
[p] In general, the IRS says you need a federal EIN if you answer “yes” to any of the following questions:
[ul]
- Do you have employees?
- Do you operate your business as a corporation or a partnership?
- Do you file employment, excise, or alcohol, tobacco and firearms tax returns?
- Are taxes withheld from non-resident alien employees, other than on income?
- Are you a sole proprietor with a Keogh pension plan?
- Are you involved with trusts, IRAs, estates, non-profit organizations, farmers’ co-operatives or plan administrators?
[p] Owners of sole proprietorships often choose to use their social security numbers as their EIN. Even if your business already has an assigned EIN, you’ll probably need a new one if you incorporate, take on partners, purchase or inherit a business, or if you are subjected to a bankruptcy proceeding. To acquire an EIN, you must apply to the IRS using Form SS-4. Forms are available at IRS and social security offices, or you can apply on-line through the IRS website. Some states also require a state EIN.
