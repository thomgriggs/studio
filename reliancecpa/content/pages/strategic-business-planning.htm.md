[h3] What Is Strategic Business Planning and How Can Your Tampa Accountants Assist You?
[img] alt="Tampa Accountants Help with Strategic Business Planning" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/strategic-business-planning.jpg
[p] True to its name, strategic business planning is creating a strategy for an area of your business and then following that plan for a certain period of time. Different than a general business plan (which is a detailed outline that you may put together before your business opens), strategic business planning typically focuses on a single area of an already established business. Therefore, if your business is in the Tampa Bay area, a strategic business plan may be your strategy to roll out a new product or service in Hillsborough County. Another example of a strategic business planning might be how to increase your sales force from three salespeople to 10 in the next year.
[p] While strategic business planning is essential to give your company clear direction and goals, you should not spend all of your time—or all of your employees’ time—in the planning process, as this takes valuable hours away from more productive and profitable activities.
