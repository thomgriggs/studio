Keep the following points in mind as you make a decision about who will prepare your taxes:
[ul]
- A preparer must sign the return and fill in the areas specifically set aside for the preparer.
- Always review the completed return, including name, address, Social Security number, and all other relevant information.
- Never sign a blank or incomplete tax return.
- Beware of tax preparers who charge based on a percentage of the potential tax return.
- Steer clear of preparers who claim they can guarantee a larger return than other preparers.
