[h3] A Dental CPA Talks About Establishing a Mobile Marketing Strategy for Your Dental Practice
[img] alt="Mobile Marketing" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/Mobile-Marketing-300x200.jpg
[p] In this age of smart phones and tablet-based commerce, no business – not even a dental practice – can afford to ignore the importance of marketing to the mobile audience. There are many reasons why a dental practice needs to be aware of how it is perceived by the mobile audience, and it all starts with convenience. Think about how you use your iPhone or Droid-based smart phone when you’re out and about. If you need to find a place of business, what do you do? You plug in the name of the company in whatever map application you favor, and you follow the directions.
[p] Of course, it’s not only about making your brick-and-mortar location simple to find. A dental CPA from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can explain how a sound mobile and [Internet marketing](http://reliancecpa.com/dental-internet-marketing) strategy ties in with your overall marketing strategy. This can include organic search engine optimization (SEO), pay-per-click advertising, billboard advertising, print advertising, in-office notices, and more. When it comes to formulating a mobile strategy, the key is allowing your patients and potential patients to learn and interact as much as possible with the practice. The utility of a mobile presence can include:
[ul]
- Setting appointments
- Staff biographies
- Insurance information
- Service descriptions
- Pricing information
- Mobile-specific coupons
- Linking to the practice’s social media sites (Facebook, Twitter, etc.)
- And much more
