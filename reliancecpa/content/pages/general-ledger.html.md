[h3] A CPA in Tampa Discusses the Importance of a General Ledger
[img] alt="Tampa CPA General Ledger" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/general-ledger.gif
[p] Every business, big or small, will keep a general ledger – it is the central heart of a company’s financial records. The general ledger’s main purpose is to provide a record of debits (outgoing money) and credits (incoming money) and reconcile the two. It is a long-term record, a way to keep track of the financial history of a business. In addition to being the primary document used during an internal audit, the general ledger serves as the anchor document for subledgers that document items such as cash, accounts receivable, accounts payable, and more.
