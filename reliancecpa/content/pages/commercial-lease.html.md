[h3] A Tampa CPA Discusses Finding the Best Commercial Lease for Your Business
[img] alt="Tampa CPA" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/commercial-lease.gif
[p] One of the most important considerations for keeping your business on the path to profitability is finding the right commercial lease for your company. Not only must it be affordable; a commercial lease must never become an impediment to sound business. But how best to know beforehand whether your commercial lease is the right one for you?
[p] A [Tampa CPA](http://www.reliancecpa.com/) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can provide all the research and analysis necessary to determine exactly what should be included in your commercial lease – and what should be negotiated. For example, would you accept a longer term (more years) for a lower payment? Or would a shorter term be more beneficial? Among the many items to consider within a commercial lease are:
[ul]
- Rent – including built-in escalations
- Length of the lease
- Lease renewal conditions
- Tenant responsibility for utilities, including phone, electricity, and water
- Tenant responsibility for maintenance
- Required security deposit, if any
- Zoning assurances
- Sublease stipulations
- Termination stipulations for the landlord and the tenant
