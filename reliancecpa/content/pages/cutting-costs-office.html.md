[h3] A Tampa CPA Discusses Office Frugality: Cutting Costs in the Workplace
[img] alt="Cutting Costs at the Office" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/cutting-costs-office.gif
[p] No matter how much a business is thriving, it’s always wise to keep a close eye on the big and small expenses that constantly assault the bottom line. That means taking the time to annually review costs for big-ticket items, like insurance and travel. But it also means scrutinizing the costs of day-to-day activities in the office, which can be an almost invisible drain on monetary resources.
[p] A [Tampa CPA](http://www.reliancecpa.com/) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help make sure your company is getting the most out of its revenue streams. Accurate record-keeping is a start, whether it’s through QuickBooks or another financial software. Smart tax planning is another good way to save money, and Reliance can help with that, as well.
[p] But there are other, simple steps a company owner or office manager can take to keep costs to a minimum, including:
[ul]
- Use refurbished office furniture, rather than buying new every time.
- Don’t leave lights on in unused rooms.
- Store files electronically, rather than in printed form, when possible.
- If printing is necessary, avoid the excessive use of color ink, if possible.
- Upgrade software and memory capacity on the company network, rather than buying new computers every year.
