[h3] A Tampa CPA Discusses What Causes Small Businesses to Fail
[img] alt="CPA Tampa" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/small-business-failure.gif
[p] Perhaps the two most egregious errors committed by small business owners are a lack of a long-term company vision and a failure to establish clear goals and objectives. Going into a business endeavor blindly is naïve at best, reckless at worst, and a sure way to cause the business to fail.
[p] A [Tampa CPA](http://www.reliancecpa.com//index.html) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help businesses, old and new, establish a long-term vision. And, using that vision as a guide, a small business advisor from Reliance can work hand-in-hand with company executives to establish clear goals and determine the best course of action to reach those objectives. A CPA from Reliance can also help a small business avoid the following potential pitfalls:
[ul]
- Misunderstanding of customer needs and desires
- Failure to give the competition enough credit
- Inadequate financial planning
- Lack of strong leadership
- Ineffective procedures and systems
- Absence of critical business skills
- Inflexibility
- Failure to communicate the plan
