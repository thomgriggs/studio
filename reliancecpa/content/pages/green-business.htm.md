[h3] Your CPA in Tampa Discusses Green Business in Florida
[img] alt="CPA in Tampa - Green Business" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/greenbusiness.gif
[p] More and more business owners in the greater Tampa Bay area have begun to incorporate environmentally responsible, or “green,” practices in the workplace. To this end, the state of Florida uses tax incentives, rebates and zoning allowances to encourage businesses to conserve energy and water, as well as to use and/or donate recyclable material.
[p] Your [CPA in Tampa](http://www.reliancecpa.com/) at Reliance Consulting, LLC can help you establish best management practices for your company in order to take advantage of the many benefits available to green businesses in Florida.
[p] In addition to government incentives that can help save you money, operating your Florida business in an environmentally responsible way can provide many other benefits, such as:
[ul]
- Savings on air conditioning and water costs
- Protection from escalating fossil fuel costs
- Access to the growing market of green-conscious consumers
- Better employee morale and higher employee retention
- Compliance with strengthened building code laws
[p] Nearly every form of business can take a green approach, but it’s important to understand the benefits and potential sacrifices. It’s also important to know the difference between management practices that are only purported to be green, and practices that actually do help your company establish authentic green credentials.
