Participation in trade shows can be expensive and requires thorough planning, which might make you wonder: Is a trade show worth the time, effort and expense, especially in a down cycle? Every situation is different, but generally, the answer is yes.

Potential customers will be there, checking out the latest trends. And you’ll want to take advantage of the kind of face time you might not get with even a hundred routine sales calls.
