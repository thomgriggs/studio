[h3] A CPA in Tampa Explains the Importance of Online Reputation Management for Small Businesses
[img] alt="Tampa CPA" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/home-based-business.gif
[p] Most business owners are fully conscious of building and sustaining their reputations within their chosen industries. “Real-world” relationships have always been important determinants of a company’s standing in the community, and they always will play a vital role. However, it would be a mistake – perhaps fatal to the well-being of the business – to focus solely on face-to-face relationships while neglecting a company’s on-line reputation. Even if the company’s website exists only as a source of information (as opposed to a portal for online commerce), failure to properly position yourself online can severely damage the overall reputation of the business.
[p] A CPA in Tampa from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help business owners develop a strategy that combines the traditional means of building a solid reputation with the emerging realm of online reputation management. This means becoming aware of the terms potential customers use to search for your products or services, and making sure that when they find your company online, the information they find is truthful, compelling, and presents you and your company in the best possible light.
[p] Why is online reputation management important? Because if you don’t manage yourself, you are vulnerable to potential negative attacks by competitors, disgruntled former employees, or customers who – for whatever reason – were not completely satisfied with your product or service in the past. This not only applies to the company itself; business owners are popular targets for negative online attacks. So, it is just as important to protect the online reputation of the person who is the company’s public face as the reputation of the business itself.
