[h3] A CPA in Tampa Explains What ‘Voluntary’ Means Regarding Federal Income Tax
[img] alt="Tampa CPA" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/voluntary-tax.gif
[p] The first federal income tax was established in the United States during the Civil War, initiating the forerunner of today’s Internal Revenue Service. In 1913, the 16th Amendment to the U.S. Constitution formalized the tax system by giving Congress the legal authority to tax income. Both of these actions were taken by elected representatives of the citizens of the U.S., and, as such, were explicitly performed with the consent of the governed.
[p] In this sense, the term “voluntary” is used to describe the nature of America’s system of income taxation. It refers to a system of allowing taxpayers to initially determine the amount to be taxed, rather than the government determining the amount from the outset. Not paying taxes, and not filing a tax return, is against federal law.
