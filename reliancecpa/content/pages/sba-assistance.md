[h3] A CPA in Tampa Helps You Find Out if Your Company is Eligible for Financial Assistance from the U.S. Small Business Administration
[img] alt="SBA Assistance" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/SBA-Assistance-300x200.jpg
[p] You company might be eligible to seek loans, grants, and other forms of funding from the U.S. Small Business Administration. This source of financial assistance can be used to help with cash flow, to fund research and development, to pay for equipment or facility improvements, or a number of other business endeavors. Most businesses are eligible to apply for this kind of financial help, but there are certain restrictions.
[p] A [CPA in Tampa](http://reliancecpa.com) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help your company develop a budget that will put it on the path to profitability. One of our experienced business consultants can also help you determine whether you are eligible for financial assistance from the U.S. Small Business Administration, if you would like to apply. In general, financial assistance is available for companies that:
[ul]
- Operate for a profit
- Are engaged in, or propose to do business in, the U.S. or its possessions
- Have reasonable owner equity to invest
- Have already used or sought alternative financing options, including personal assets, to raise capital
