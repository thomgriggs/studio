[h3] A Tampa CPA Explains Why Tax Planning is a Year-Round Endeavor
[img] alt="Tax Planning Year-Round" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/Tax-Planning-Year-Round-300x200.jpg
[p] If you found yourself in a panic this year as the tax deadline approached, perhaps now is a good time to start planning for next April. In fact, there’s no “perhaps” about it – tax planning is a year-round endeavor. Year-round planning certainly helps you avoid the frantic scramble of pulling together all of your tax information at the last minute. More importantly, a smart tax plan implemented well in advance of the deadline might just save you a lot of money.
[p] A [Tampa CPA](http://reliancecpa.com) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help you develop a tax plan now that will work for you all year long. Ignoring your potential deductions and credits until the very last minute is a huge mistake, because something might be overlooked if tax preparation is rushed. On the other hand, a little forethought will save you a headache in April. For instance, it only takes a moment to file records on expenses, but that moment could pay off big at tax time. Whether you use online financial software or banking, or you file paper receipts in a secure, safe place at home or your office, here are a few expenses that could have an impact on your taxes:
[ul]
- Medical expenses
- Tuition fees
- Child care costs
- Charitable donations
- Political contributions
- Child support payments
- Alimony payments
- And more
[p] It makes absolutely no sense to procrastinate when it comes to tax planning. The more you pay attention throughout the year, the less stress you will experience at tax time.
