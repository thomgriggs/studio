[h3] A CPA in Tampa Explains What Velocity Means in the Financial World
[img] alt="Velocity" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/velocity-300x210.jpg
[p] Everyone knows that velocity is speed in a given direction, and many people are familiar with the term from high school physics class. Yet, there is another very important use of the term velocity, and it has to do with money. Specifically, it is how often a particular dollar changes hands over a specific amount of time. Financial velocity is a useful measurement of how strong the economy is, and often is used as a comparison for the gross domestic product or the money supply.
