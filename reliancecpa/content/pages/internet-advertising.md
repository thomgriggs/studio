Either you couldn’t care less about the state of the custom cowboy boot industry, or you have not been paying attention to the potential power of Internet advertising. Of course, these two are not mutually exclusive. Plenty of people neither care about custom cowboy boots, nor Internet advertising. But anyone with a small business should care a great deal about the Paul Bond Boots story, because it is a lesson in the power of Internet advertising.
[p] A [CPA in Tampa](http://reliancecpa.com) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help small business owners determine how best to market their goods or services. In 2009, CNBC featured Paul Bond Boots as a company that had made the most of Google’s still-evolving AdWords feature. The custom boot company bought just a few select terms and began to show up in paid search queries all over the world. Almost overnight, they increased their business by 20 percent. Their custom cowboy boots, which had been marketed previously only through word of mouth, became a worldwide sensation.
