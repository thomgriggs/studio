[h3] A CPA in Tampa Discusses Starting an On-line Business
[img] alt="Tampa CPA" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/on-line-business.gif
[p] If you are one of the millions of business owners worldwide who would like to take advantage of the growth opportunities offered by Internet commerce (e-commerce), it’s important not to jump in blindly. Just as with any brick-and-mortar business, starting an on-line business requires a thorough understanding of the rules and regulations, as well as the sound principals that help any business grow.
[p] A [CPA in Tampa](http://www.reliancecpa.com//index.html) from Reliance Consulting can help you take the first step to e-commerce profitability by ensuring that the “business” side of your on-line business is on a firm footing. After all, all those Web shoppers out there aren’t going to care about your spiffy, entertaining website if they aren’t provided the service or product they’re after. A Reliance business consultant will take care of the financial details – payroll services, cash flow management, tax planning, etc. – while you focus on production and the big picture. Once you have the business side in hand, here are the basic steps to taking your commerce on-line:
[ul]
- Prepare a business plan to help crystallize your vision
- Register a domain name
- Select a Web host
- Design your website
- Optimize and market your website
- Learn and comply with on-line business regulations and compliance information
- Learn and comply with federal, state, and local tax requirements
- Learn and understand international trade laws
