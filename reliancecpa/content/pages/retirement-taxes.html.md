[img] alt="Tampa CPA" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/retirement-taxes.gif
[p] Living on a “fixed income” in retirement doesn’t mean you’ll be exempt from paying taxes. That’s why it’s important to take income taxes into account as you plan for retirement. Steps taken now can help you minimize your income tax burden later in life.

That includes preparing to answer questions about retirement income taxes, such as: Will you pay quarterly taxes? And, how much will your former company withhold from a pension payout? Sources of income that affect the amount of income tax you’ll pay in retirement include:
[ul]
- Some retirement account distributions
- Social Security distributions
- Pension payments
- Other income, including inheritance or other monetary windfalls
