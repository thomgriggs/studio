[h3] A CPA in Tampa Can Help You Define Your Personal Financial Goals
[img] alt="Personal Financial Goals" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/Personal-Financial-Goals-300x200.jpg
[p] No matter what your personal financial circumstances may be, there is virtually no chance that they will improve unless you make a plan that takes into account how things might look a year from now, five years from now, 10 years from now, and well into the future. You can certainly sit down with your significant other and try to figure out the future on your own, but there is an easier way to make sure that you and your loved ones are protected financially in the years to come.
[p] A [CPA in Tampa](http://reliancecpa.com) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, will sit down with you and help you determine the priorities that will shape your financial future. This will not necessarily be an overnight process. Most of us are so busy in our lives that we rarely have time to give much thought to our long-term life goals. Determining these goals, however, is job No. 1 for anyone who is concerned about being secure in retirement and leaving a meaningful monetary legacy for their heirs. The kinds of questions a Reliance CPA might ask include:
[ul]
- How much longer do you want to work?
- How much traveling do you think you’ll want to do in retirement?
- What sort of pastimes do you anticipate participating in after your working days are done?
- Do you want to live in the house where you live now, or do you plan to move somewhere else in retirement?
- How much money do you anticipate needing for assisted living, should it come to that?
- And many more
[p] The answers you give to these questions might change from year to year, but knowing the answers now will give you a starting point as you plan for life in retirement. To get started, [contact](http://reliancecpa.com/contact.html) Reliance Consulting today.
