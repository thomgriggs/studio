[h3] A Tampa CPA Explains Related-Party Transactions
[img] alt="Tampa CPA" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/Related-Party-Transaction-300x200.jpg
[p] Related-party transactions are deals or arrangements between parties that are connected by a pre-existing relationship. This can include transactions between a corporation and one or more of its major shareholders, or business-related services provided to a company officer in a position of authority by an associate, business partner, or family member. According to the Internal Revenue Service, special rules apply to the sale or trade of property or goods between parties that are related in some way.
