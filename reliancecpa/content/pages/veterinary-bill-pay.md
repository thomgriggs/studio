[h2] The Advantages of Outsourcing Your Bill Pay
[p] Using an offsite accountant to pay your bills can have a number of benefits for you and your practice.
[ul]
- **Saves you time:** Staying on top of your bills can require hours every month. As a busy veterinarian and small business owner, you simply do not have that much time at your disposal. When you work with an offsite accountant, you will spend about 15 to 30 minutes a week dealing with bills and electronic transactions.
- **Saves you money:** Hiring a full-time bookkeeper can cost your practice quite a bit of money. By outsourcing your bill pay work, you get expert service at a much lower cost.
- **Helps you avoid late fees and calls from creditors:** With so much else on your mind, it is easy to miss a crucial financial deadline. An offsite accountant focuses exclusively on this area of your practice. Therefore, you can easily pay all of your bills on time.
- **Creates a solid financial foundation:** Often an experienced accountant will identify bookkeeping errors made in the past. Then he or she can rectify these mistakes before they wreak havoc with your finances.
- **Reduces stress:** With a secure financial footing and more time to focus on the other aspects of your practice, your life will be easier and more hassle-free.
