[h3] A Tampa CPA Discusses the First Step to Starting a Business
[img] alt="Tampa CPA" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/starting-new-business-197x300.gif
[p] Logic says the first step toward starting your own company is to write a sound, well-researched business plan. Yet, there is one extremely important consideration to take into account before thinking about the structure and strategy that are the foundation of a good business plan. Namely, are you absolutely certain that you have what it takes to own and operate your own business?
[p] A [Tampa CPA](http://www.reliancecpa.com//index.html) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help you answer that question, as well as providing all the advice you need to get your business off the ground and on the path to profitability.
[p] Let’s say you have a great idea for a business. You’ve determined that you can provide a product or service that you’re fairly certain people will want to buy. And it’s possible that your big idea is appealing enough to be the basis of a stand-alone company. How do you proceed? First, ask yourself if you’re the kind of person who can run a business.
[p] Consider the following factors as you decide:
[ul]
- Do you have the entrepreneurial know-how necessary to run a business?
- Do you have enough money to start a business?
- Do you have the willingness to put in the long hours and hard work it will take to make the business a success?
- Do you have the fortitude to face the risks and challenges inherent to any new business venture?
[p] A business consultant from Reliance can help you determine the answers to these and other questions as you decide whether your ability to provide a particular product or service is big enough to be the foundation for a new business.
