The last thing you want to do is rush into creating a new business, especially considering the attrition rate for small businesses in the U.S. is so high.

[ul]

- Where will you get seed money?
- Do you have what it takes to face the daily ups and downs of owning a business?

- Do you have the entrepreneurial know-how to create a business plan?
- Have you conducted the right kind of market research?
