[p] Doctors and other health care providers in the Tampa, FL area face many challenges as they try to keep their practices running smoothly. Dealing with serious issues such as reduced reimbursements from Medicare and insurance providers, as well as the constant threat of medical malpractice lawsuits, physicians often find themselves spending too much time worrying about financials. Sadly, this stress takes valuable time away from their primary objective—caring for patients.
[p] At Reliance Consulting, LLC, our biggest niche is serving physicians. From Tampa to Sarasota, Lakeland to Pasco, St Petersburg to Clearwater and throughout Tampa Bay, Reliance Consulting has a team of experienced CPAs and accountants with decades of experience working alongside doctors, dentists, clinics, hospitals, nursing homes, and other health care facilities. As a matter of fact, our CPAs have authored a booklet about the financial, tax, and legal matters that doctors face. You can download this valuable information for free, in Adobe .pdf format, by clicking this link: [Navigating the Business of Medicine](http://www.reliancecpa.com/navigating-the-business-of-medicine.pdf)

Reliance Consulting is here to help doctors be aware of what they are buying and help them avoid costly financial mistakes.
