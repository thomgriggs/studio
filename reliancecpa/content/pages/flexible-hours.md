[h3] A CPA in Tampa Explains the Benefits of Flexible Hours in a Small Business
[img] alt="Flexible Hours" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/Flexible-Hours-300x200.jpg
[p] There are many reasons a small, boutique firm might want to keep an office full of people on display. For one thing, visiting clients gain a certain level of trust when they see workers together under one roof. It’s a way to prove your company is more than a one-horse operation. Yet, not all companies are at their most productive when their employees are forced to commute. In fact, the drudgery of office life can be a big drain on employee morale. Because a happy employee is a productive employee, many business owners have begun to embrace the concept of flexible hours and work-at-home arrangements.
[p] A [CPA in Tampa](http://reliancecpa.com) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help you determine how best to structure your workforce. This includes making sure that that your employees are given every opportunity to enjoy a healthy work-life balance. One of the greatest obstacles to that balance is a rigid set of workday hours, during which the employee is expected to be essentially chained to his or her desk. There are plenty of cases in which that is merely a function of the job, and the work would not be completed any other way. However, in cases that allow for working at home, flex hours might be the way to go, especially for workers whose jobs are performed on computers. Flex hours give employees the freedom to perform daily personal tasks, such as taking children to the pediatrician, without fear of repercussions. As long as the work is done – and done well – employers who provide flexible work hours generally find that their employees are happier, more productive, and more loyal.
