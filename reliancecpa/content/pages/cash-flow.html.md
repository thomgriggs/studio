[img] alt="CPA Tampa" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/cash-flow.gif
[p] Cash flow is defined as the movement of cash into and out of a business. It usually is measured over a specified period of time and is a vital indicator of a company’s ability to meet its financial obligations, as well as its bottom-line profitability. Proper cash flow management also is important during times of growth, when a business needs cash to invest upfront money on materials and payroll to keep up with growing demand.
