[h3] A Dental CPA from Reliance Consulting Helps with Asset Protection Planning
[img] alt="Dental Asset Protection Planning" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/Dental-Asset-Protection-Planning-300x200.jpg
[p] Anyone who has practiced dentistry – or any form of healthcare – long enough understands how important it is to make asset protection planning a priority. We live in an increasingly litigious society, and no dental practice, no matter how competent, is ever entirely immune from being sued. It is therefore vital for owner-operators of dental practices to shield themselves from losing personal and professional assets.
[p] A [dental CPA](http://reliancecpa.com/dental-cpa) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help you set up your practice using a business model that will provide as much asset protection as possible, while also preserving the efficiency and top-notch dental care your patients demand.
[p] Asset protection planning usually starts with carrying the right kind and amount of insurance. Forms of insurance a dental practice should consider carrying include:
[ul]
- Professional liability
- General liability
- Vicarious liability
- Disciplinary coverage
- Licensure coverage
- Property coverage
- Much more
[p] Before determining what insurance coverage to purchase and how else to protect your practice and yourself, asset protection planning includes [titling your assets](http://www.reliancecpa.com/titling-dental-assets), taking [depreciation](http://www.reliancecpa.com/dental-depreciation) into account, and establishing a shield against a wide variety of risks, including:
[ul]
- Malpractice lawsuits
- Divorce
- Partner disputes
- Poor investments
- Business cycle downturns
- And more
[p] A dental CPA from Reliance Consulting can help you position yourself in the best way possible to dissuade anyone from making claims against your assets.
