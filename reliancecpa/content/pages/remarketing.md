[h3] A Tampa CPA Explains the Benefits of Online Remarketing Campaigns for Small Businesses
[img] alt="Remarketing" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/Remarketing-300x195.jpg
[p] If you have ever been browsing along on the Internet and noticed that a display ad for a website you visited recently popped up on an entirely different – and possibly unrelated – website, then you have witnessed online remarketing in action. It might seem a little off-putting, almost as if a segment of the Internet was following you around. But it is a great way for small businesses to reinforce an advertising message and generate online leads. A [Tampa CPA](http://reliancecpa.com) from Reliance Consulting, LLC, can help small business owners develop ways to use the Internet and other strategies for building a customer base through advertising. Remarketing is a relatively affordable and effective way to make the most of a relatively small advertising budget, but it is only one method for small business owners to level the playing field online. Other methods of Internet marketing include: • Pay per click • Organic search engine optimization • Social media marketing • Facebook ads • Twitter “promoted” tweets • Static Internet display advertisements • And more
[p] To stay ahead of the competition, small businesses need to take advantage of every technological innovation available at a reasonable cost. And, compared to most traditional forms of advertising, Internet marketing is extremely affordable – and effective.
