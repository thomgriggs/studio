[h3] A CPA in Tampa Reveals Resources to Explain Market and Economic Conditions
[img] alt="Tampa CPA Diversification" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/tax-diversification.gif
[p] It’s great to have an idea for a business and to pursue it with passion and gusto. However, starting or operating a company without having a firm understanding of your target market and current economic conditions is operating in a dangerous vacuum. One of the main reasons more than 50 percent of startups fail in the first five years is ignorance of the information that can make or break a company’s bottom line. It is therefore vital for business owners to know where to turn for this information.
[p] A CPA in Tampa from Reliance Consulting, LLC, is a great resource for this kind of information. Since 1984, Reliance has helped hundreds of small Florida businesses navigate a wide range of economic conditions. It starts with a plan, of course, but a plan based on no data is merely guesswork.
[p] The federal and state governments provide important data for businesses, and there are a number of organizations that track economic and market data and offer their results either on-line or through print publications. These data bases and publications include:
[ul]
- The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS Codes)
- FedStats
- Statistical Abstract of the United States
- Statistics of U.S. Businesses from U.S. Census County Business Patterns
- The Small Business Association Office of Advocacy
- STAT-USA
- American FactFinder
- Monthly Census Bureau reports
- Labor demographics from the Bureau of Labor Statistics
[p] There are many other sources of information, any one of which may contain the data that are crucial components for helping form your short- or long-term strategy.
