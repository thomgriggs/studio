[h3] A Tampa CPA Explains the Purpose of the Office of Taxpayer Burden Reduction
[img] alt="Tampa CPA" src=http://www.reliancecpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/taxpayer-burden.gif
[p] The Internal Revenue Service established the Office of Taxpayer Burden Reduction (OTBR) in 2002 in order to address the amount of time and money taxpayers spend to prepare and pay their federal taxes. Although the phrase “taxpayer burden” might seem to refer to the actual amount of income tax owed each year by a business or individual, in this case it means the obstacles – paperwork, tax preparation fees, etc. – that make it more difficult to pay what is owed in taxes.

Fortunately, the IRS acknowledges the fact that its processes could be improved and simplified by providing the public the ability to submit burden reduction proposals to the OTBR, which focuses its efforts on four main areas:
[ul]
- Simplifying taxpayer forms and IRS publications
- Streamlining policies and procedures within the internal structure of the IRS
- Promoting less burdensome laws and rulings
- Developing new, more accurate ways to assess taxpayer burden
