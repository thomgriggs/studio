hero 01-hero.html

Purpose
Opener. Home variant (a background_video slot): the original Broadsheet hero, full-bleed looping video with a dark scrim, two-line h1, lead and a primary plus an outlined button. Tier variant (a tier slot): 7/5 split, the tier eyebrow in its territory colour with a short thin rule, h1, tagline, buttons, and a photo (the concept's own photograph for that tier). Slots
eyebrow, title, tagline, tier, background_video, image, actions[text, href].
Squarespace 7.1
Home: Section with background video (fall back to its image), Heading 1, Text, two Buttons; the scrim is the section overlay (opacity only, not the gradient) and the autoplay keep-alive script is not possible, see SQUARESPACE.md. Tier: two-column Section, Heading + Text + Buttons beside an Image block; the eyebrow is a small Text block (territory colour from the page's custom CSS block).
