How Payroll Processing by a CPA in Tampa Can Help Your Small Business

AccountingFrom the Reliance Consulting team

The reports, the withholding, the laws, the checks … payroll can be a lengthy chore that consumes a large portion of a Tampa Bay small business owner’s time. It is estimated that payroll processing takes about a day’s worth of time every month—totaling 12 days a year. Clearly, this is time that a business owner could use for generating sales, launching new products, and other profitable ventures.

When you consider the risk of making a costly legal mistake with payroll — and the time that payroll takes away from more productive activities — it just makes sense for Tampa area business owners to hire a CPA in Tampa to handle all of their payroll processing. Much more than a simple payroll processing company, a CPA will have extensive background and knowledge to also advise business owners on ways to grow their company through smarter financial decisions.

Reliance Consulting, LLC has worked with hundreds of Tampa area businesses over our 25-plus year history, and during that time, we have provided flawless payroll services. When you outsource your payroll to a CPA in Tampa from Reliance Consulting, LLC, you can look forward to the following benefits:

  • Saving time—You won’t have to worry about data processing, looking at tax tables, writing checks, calculating overtime, sending out W-2 forms on time, and all the other paperwork that goes into payroll. With the time you save, you can concentrate on improving your sales, products, and services—or maybe you can take a few days off to recharge.
  • Saving money—Hiring a CPA in Tampa from Reliance Consulting is more cost effective than hiring an employee or employees to handle your payroll. In addition, a CPA will also be there to provide a wealth of other accounting services that can help you earn and keep more of your money.
  • Preventing errors—Managing your own payroll carries a significant amount of risk. If you make a mistake, you could be facing fines and possibly even criminal charges. Let the experts at Reliance Consulting keep up with the ever changing laws of payroll processing. We will make sure your payroll runs smoothly.

Another advantage of our payroll processing service is our payroll reports which will provide you with valuable information for running your business and planning for the future. For more information about how payroll processing by a CPA in Tampa from Reliance Consulting can be one of the best decisions you’ve ever made, contact us for a free consultation today.
