site-header templates/_nav.html

Purpose
The 34px ink utility bar (tagline, Client login in lime) over the sticky 86px white header: logo, Services (the mega flyout: a rounded white panel, one column per tier with heading, one-line description, links and "See all"; the Consulting column adds the industry examples), Resources and About as simple dropdowns, Contact and the coral Get Started button. CSS-only hover and keyboard focus on desktop; at 1000px and below a Menu button opens a full-width panel with accordions (the original had no mobile menu). The active tier shows in its territory colour as a thin underline. Hover the header of this page to see it.
Slots
content/nav.json: menu (folders, fallback), mega.columns[tier, heading, description, accent, overview, links[label, url, summary], secondary], links (Contact, Client Login), button.
Squarespace 7.1
The flyout is a mega-menu plugin (Will Myers, Launch Hub Studio or SQSP Themes) rendering a normal page of three Section columns: Heading 3 (link), Text, a Text block of links, a "See all" link. Folders (Resources, About) are native dropdowns. The same three tiers as top-level folders are the mobile and no-plugin fallback (nav.json `menu`). Header: logo left, one Button (Get Started, coral), Client Login in the announcement bar (the native Announcement Bar holds the tagline and a link). Territory underline = custom CSS. Sticky header is a native setting.

site-footer templates/_footer.html

Purpose
The original's dark footer: white-plate logo with the Get Started button, then the link grid (three tiers with a thin territory rule, contact, then Resources, About, Legal and Follow, headings in lime) and the hairline copyright row. This page's own footer is the live sample below.
Slots
content/footer.json: columns, contact (address, phone, email, directions), social, legal links, extras, client_login, copyright.
Squarespace 7.1
Footer sections: Text blocks of links (or Navigation-style lists), a Text block for address and phone, a Social Links block if wanted. The territory rule above the tier columns is custom CSS.
