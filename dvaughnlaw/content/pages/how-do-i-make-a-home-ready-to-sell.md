[h1] How Do I Make A Home Ready To Sell?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/hasm7d1h6z
[p] As we show you in this video, start several months before the property is made available. Look through the eyes of a buyer
[ul]
- What needs to be cleaned?
- Repainted?
- Repaired?
- Or tossed?
[p] Ask yourself – or a friend If you were buying this house what would you want to see?
[p] The goal is to show a home that looks good makes the most of its assets like space and location and attracts as many buyers and as much demand as possible.
[p] Allow yourself enough lead time – not just a day or two – to make the most of the sale. And get help from a real estate agent – early.
