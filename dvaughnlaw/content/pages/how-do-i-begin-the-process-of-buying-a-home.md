[h1] How Do I Begin The Process Of Buying A Home?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/72uzv0ula9
[p] Remember these pointers from the video: start by thinking about your situation.
[ul]
- Are you ready to buy a home?
- How much can you afford in a monthly mortgage payment?
- How much space do you need?
- What areas of town do you like?
[p] After you answer these questions, make a “To Do” list and start doing casual research. Talk to friends and family, drive through neighborhoods, and look in the “Homes” section of the newspaper, or online.
