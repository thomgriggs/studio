[h1] What Are Discount Points?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/55102bnft3
[p] Discount points allow you to lower your interest rate. While this video simplifies things to help you remember, “points” are essentially prepaid interest with each point equaling 1% of the total loan amount.
[p] Generally, for each point paid on a 30-year mortgage the interest rate is reduced by 1/8 (or.125) of a percentage point.
[p] When shopping for loans, ask lenders for an interest rate with 0 points and then see how much the rate decreases with each point paid.
[p] Discount points are smart if you plan to stay in a home for some time since they can lower the monthly loan payment.
[p] Points are tax deductible when you purchase a home and you may be able to negotiate for the seller to pay for some of them.
