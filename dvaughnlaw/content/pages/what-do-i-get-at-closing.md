[h1] What Do I Get At Closing?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/bi9izzbvx6
[p] For most real estate loans, you will receive a Closing Disclosure 3 business days before loan consummation – which frequently happens at the closing meeting. At the meeting itself you should receive a copy of your Mortgage Note – your obligation to repay- your Mortgage or Deed of Trust the binding Sales Contract and – in some states – you may get the keys to your new home.
