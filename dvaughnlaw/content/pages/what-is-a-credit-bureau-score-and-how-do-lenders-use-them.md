[h1] What Is A Credit Bureau Score And How Do Lenders Use Them?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/vkym0nc4ud
[p] As we show you in this video, a credit bureau score, or “credit score” is a number based upon your credit history that represents the possibility that you will be unable to repay a loan.
[p] Lenders use it to determine your ability to qualify for a mortgage loan.
[p] The better the score, the better your chances are of getting a favorable loan.
[p] Know your score and ensure that lenders have current information about it.
