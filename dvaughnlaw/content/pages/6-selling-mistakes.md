[h1] 6 Selling Mistakes
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/ojf3pzph7e
[p] If you’re selling, don’t do these things – take some notes from the video! **1. Don’t Sell Before The House Is Ready.**
[p] If it doesn’t present well, it won’t sell well.
[p] **2. Don’t Over-Improve**
[p] People buy houses in neighborhoods.
[p] If yours is so “improved” that it sticks out you’re hurting your chances at selling.
[p] **3. Hire Wrong**
[p] Make your agent choice for business reasons.
[p] Personal relationships matter, but experience and expertise will determine financial success in your sale.
[p] **4. Don’t Hide Anything**
[p] Covering up or ‘failing to mention’ real problems doesn’t work.
[p] State disclosure laws are strict and you can be sued after the sale for anything that should have been made clear.
[p] **5. Don’t Rush**
[p] You should know about your mortgage, including pre-payment penalties your market conditions and trends and your options for your next home before jumping on the market.
[p] **6. Don’t Get Too Emotional**
[p] Your attachment to your house and your own financial needs
[p] don’t really matter in the transaction.
[p] If you can’t set them aside the sale won’t go as you’d like it to.
[p] Remember – it was your home but to the buyer it’s as a house.
