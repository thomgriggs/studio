[h1] Could My Loan Costs Exceed The Loan Estimate?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/i9b8sl8uie
[p] Yes, within defined limits.
[p] Service charges for which YOU shop and select a provider may change; the creditor is NOT responsible for providers who are NOT on their written list.
[p] In addition, prepaid interest, property insurance premiums and escrow or reserve deposits may change without legal tolerance limits.
[p] Charges for recording services, and 3rd-party services ON the creditor list, grouped together may not exceed the Loan Estimate total for the same charges by more than 10%.
[p] Transfer taxes, fees paid to the creditor, mortgage broker or an affiliate of either and fees paid to a 3rd party for services the creditor does NOT permit you to shop are ZERO tolerance and must match the Loan Estimate.
