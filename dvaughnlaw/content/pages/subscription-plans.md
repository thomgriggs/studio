[h1] Subscription Plans
[h2] Subscription Plans
[text] **Overview:** These subscription plans provide an affordable way to retain legal services on a limited basis. Each plan is designed for different levels of real estate investors, landlords, and property managers. Monthly fees are earned upon receipt, non-refundable, and automatically billed.

[ul:accordion]
- Registered Agent filed with State Department & Sunbiz listing
- All Registered Agent mail will be forwarded to Company per email
- Complimentary call when papers are served on Company

- **Limited Scheduled Zoom/Calls:** Access to brief, pre-scheduled consultations with priority access to an attorney on staff.
- **Flat-Fee Services:** Eligibility for select legal services at discounted flat rates.
- **Knowledge Base Access:** Access to a continuously expanding library of real estate legal resources, FAQs and a monthly legal newsletter.

[h3:accordion] Terms and Conditions for Legal Questions Retainer Subscription Plans
[p:accordion] Designed for individuals, families, and business owners needing comprehensive estate planning and asset protection.
[ol:accordion]
1. **12-Month Commitment** All subscription plans require a 12-month commitment. Early termination of the subscription prior to the completion of the 12-month period will result in a cancellation fee.
2. **Eviction Requests** Eviction services do not roll over and must be utilized within the month in which they are allocated. Unused eviction requests will not carry over to the following month.
3. **Response Time** The Firm commits to responding to your legal inquiries within 24-48 hours of receiving a message from the client, in the client portal, during regular business hours (Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM). All phone calls with firm attorneys will be scheduled in advance. Subscribers will receive priority access to an attorney on staff, which includes expedited responses to messages during business hours.
4. **Leases Utilized by the Firm** The Firm will provide updates and compliance checks For units that utilize the Firm’s leases only to ensure that your leases remain up-to-date with applicable laws and regulations.
5. **Client Portal Access** Subscribers will have access to a client portal, where legal questions will be answered, and communication with the Firm can be managed efficiently. All messages must be communicated with firm attorneys through the client portal.
6. **Payment and Fees** Payment is due monthly as outlined in the subscription plan. Failure to make timely payments may result in suspension of services until the account is brought current. Payments are processed automatically on a recurring monthly basis. If there is an issue with the payment method, the client will be notified, and services may be suspended until the issue is resolved.
7. **Confidentiality** All communications between the Firm and the client are confidential and will be handled in accordance with applicable confidentiality and attorney-client privilege rules. However, the Firm is not responsible for maintaining confidentiality if the client shares information with third parties outside of the Firm.
8. **Attorney Availability** While the Firm commits to responding to client inquiries within the stated timeframe, attorney availability may vary based on workload, scheduled time off, or unforeseen circumstances. In such cases, the Firm will notify the client as soon as possible.
9. **Scope of Services** These services are limited to the scope defined in the specific retainer plan. Any services beyond what is included may be subject to additional fees and require a separate agreement.
10. **No Guarantee of Outcome** The Firm cannot guarantee any specific legal outcomes or results. All legal matters are subject to a variety of factors beyond the Firm’s control, including judicial decisions and the facts of each case.
11. **Force Majeure** The Firm will not be liable for any failure or delay in the performance of its obligations under these terms caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, pandemics, labor strikes, or government restrictions.
12. **Termination** Both the Firm and the client have the right to terminate the agreement after the 12-month commitment period with 30 days’ written notice. If the client wishes to terminate early, a cancellation fee may apply. All unused services will be forfeited upon termination.
13. **Indemnification** The client agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Firm from any claims, losses, or damages resulting from the client’s misuse of the services, including but not limited to wrongful evictions or violations of applicable law.
14. **Legal Advice Limitations** The services provided under these plans are not intended to replace full legal representation for complex matters. Should a matter require extensive legal representation, a separate agreement and fee structure will be discussed.
15. **Client Responsibilities** Clients are responsible for providing accurate and timely information to the Firm. Failure to do so may impact the Firm’s ability to provide accurate legal advice or services.
16. **Modifications** The Firm reserves the right to modify these terms and conditions at any time. Subscribers will be notified of any changes, and continued use of the services will constitute acceptance of the updated terms.
17. **Limitation of Liability** The Firm will not be liable for any indirect, special, or consequential damages arising from the use of the Legal Questions Retainer service.
