[h1] How Large A Down Payment Do I Need?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/axrta9slj3
[p] There are mortgage options now available that only require a down payment of 5% or less of the purchase price. You’ll see some pictures in this video to help you remember later – the larger the down payment, the less you have to borrow and the more equity you’ll have.
[p] Mortgages with less than a 20% down payment generally require a mortgage insurance policy to secure the loan.
[p] When considering the size of your down payment consider that you’ll also need money for closing costs moving expenses, and – possibly – repairs and decorating.
