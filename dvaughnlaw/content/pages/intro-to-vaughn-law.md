[h1] Intro to Vaughn Law
[p] As you walk down the bustling streets of Corey Ave., filled with retail shops, restaurants and bars, you will see the teal accents on the corner of Corey Ave. and Blind Pass Rd. of the new law office for Vaughn Law. Vaughn Law is a boutique law firm that (began its roots 5 years ago in St. Petersburg) focuses on Real Estate Law. We handle real estate transactions and closings; contract preparation and negotiation; and litigation. With the opening of this second location, Vaughn Law is expanding its services to offer Estate Planning and Probate.
[p] Vaughn Law has an amazing team, made up of twelve members, to assist you with your real estate legal needs. Danielle Vaughn, Owner and managing attorney of Vaughn Law, is an expert in real estate law. She is a board-certified real estate attorney and has a Master of Laws in Real Property Development. Vaughn Law has two associate attorneys to also assist you with your real estate questions and issues.
[p] So you might ask yourself, why Vaughn Law? Vaughn Law is committed to providing knowledgeable, straightforward and timely legal services through consistent communication with all clients. This means you will get real and honest legal advice, even if it is not exactly what you want to hear. The Mission of Vaughn Law is to educate clients on their rights and support them in resolving their real estate issues and planning for their future estate needs, to empower them to make the best decisions possible to further their ultimate goals. At every consultation, we focus on providing clients with all of their options to allow them to make educated decisions. And with this column, you will see us feature a new area of Real Estate law and Estate planning so we can educate and inform you on how you can best protect yourself with one of the largest assets you own. From your first call until we complete your matter, you’ll feel genuinely supported through the entire process. We combine top-notch legal expertise, superior accountability, communication, and great care for you and your family members involved in the process. Said another way, we do what we say we will do because we truly care! That will show through each step of your journey with us. We know you have a lot of choices, and when you choose Vaughn Law, we show our gratitude through great service.
