[h1] Understanding Your Loan: Cash And Transaction Summaries
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/6c5em0poro
[p] Page 3 of your Closing Disclosure will compare cash requirements from your Loan Estimate to your actual final charges. If “Did this change?” is “YES” notes for changed sections should be provided.
[p] The bottom line final “Cash to Close” is the money you will need in-hand in three business days.
[p] If your transaction has a Seller the summary table will show a line by line comparison of Borrower to Seller transaction details.
[p] If there is no Seller you may see a Payoffs and Payments table instead.
[p] Review the summary tables to understand the transaction and your financial commitments at loan consummation.
