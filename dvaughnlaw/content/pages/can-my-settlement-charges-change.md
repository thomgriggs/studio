[h1] Can My Settlement Charges Change?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/nouv4njnrh
[p] Yes, if circumstances change, such as:
[ul]
- a natural disaster damages the property or affects closing costs
- the title insurer providing the estimate goes out of business during underwriting
- new information on you or the transaction affecting settlement is discovered.
[p] If any of these events change 3rd-party charges beyond the 10% tolerance limit creditors may issue a revised Loan Estimate.
[p] If a creditor issues a Loan Estimate they are presumed to have collected all 6 pieces of required information. They may not claim a change in circumstances by receiving one of these pieces of information AFTER issuing a Loan Estimate.
