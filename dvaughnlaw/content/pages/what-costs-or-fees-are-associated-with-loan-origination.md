[h1] What Costs or Fees Are Associated With Loan Origination?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/1hlz83ejdk
[p] Yes, loan origination involves costs and fees. As you’ll see in the video, when you turn in your application you’ll be required to pay a loan application fee to cover the costs of underwriting the loan. This fee pays for the home appraisal a copy of your credit report and any additional charges that may be necessary. The application fee is generally non-refundable.
