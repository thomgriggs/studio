[h1] Understanding Your Loan Estimate: Terms, Payments and Closing Costs
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/ttim6rll5r
[p] The first page of your Loan Disclosure shows the Loan Terms Projected Payments and Costs at Closing.
[p] The Loan Amount, of course is the total you are borrowing. But the Interest Rate alone doesn’t represent all of your borrowing costs. The APR figure on Page 3 shows that.
[p] Likewise, Monthly Principal & Interest aren’t the complete amount you will actually PAY each month.
[p] The Projected Payments figures add other costs, such as Mortgage Insurance Estimated Escrow, Taxes, Insurances and Assessment to show the approximate amount you will pay each month, over time.
[p] The Estimated Closing Costs are directly loan-related. while the Estimated Cash to Close adds other known closing costs to tell you the estimated cash you’ll need to have to close this loan.
