[h1] How Can I Keep Track Of All The Homes I See?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/09jwxt28ux
[p] There are some great tips in this video, like:
[p] if possible, take photographs of each house: the outside, the major rooms, the yard and extra features that you like or ones you see as potential problems.
[p] Write things down as you go. And don’t hesitate to return for a second look.
[p] Use the HUD Home Scorecard (www.hud.gov/buying/checklist.pdf) to organize your photos and notes for each house.
