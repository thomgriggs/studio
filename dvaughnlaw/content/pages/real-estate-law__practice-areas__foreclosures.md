[h3] What is Foreclosure?
[p] Foreclosure is the process in which the lender files an action with the court to force the sale of a piece of property, which is the security of a mortgage held by the lender, in order to pay the debt owed to the lender.
[p] In Florida, once the lender obtains a foreclosure judgment, the property is sold at an auction. Typically, the auction is held online. The proceeds from the sale are used to pay the lender and satisfy the debt owed.
[h3] FAQ
[h5:accordion] Can I stop a foreclosure?
[text:accordion] Yes, by paying off the entire balance of the mortgage, including interest, late fees, attorney’s fees and court costs. Payment must be made in full. You must confirm the payoff balance before remitting payment to the lender or their attorney.
[h5:accordion] How long does a foreclosure take in Florida?
[text:accordion] Depends. It may be as quick as a few months and it may continue for a year or even longer.
[h5:accordion] Can I reduce my loan payment?
[text:accordion] Sometimes. The lender may offer a loan modification or a temporary payment reduction. It is a good idea to investigate these options, but many are temporary fixes that may have a huge long-term impact. Make sure you know what you have agreed to before signing any documents!
[h5:accordion] What if there are additional funds left over after the lender is paid in full?
[text:accordion] The borrower may be entitled to the surplus funds after the foreclosure sale; however, the borrower must file a claim against the surplus funds within a specified time frame.
[h5:accordion] What if the sale of the property was not enough to satisfy the debt?
[text:accordion] You may be responsible for the remaining balance of the mortgage.
[h5:accordion] Who pays for the Foreclosure?
[text:accordion] The borrower is responsible for the fees associated with the foreclosure. The fees are added to the loan amount, interest, and any late fees, this is the amount owed to the lender once the property is sold at the auction.
[h5:accordion] What if the property is not worth the amount owed to the lender?
[text:accordion] The lender may obtain title to the property at the foreclosure sale, if no one else bids on it.
