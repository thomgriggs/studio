[h1] What Does Ability To Repay Mean?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/ch9gvdv6h1
[p] What are the “Ability to repay” rules about?
[p] In a nutshell, as this video shows, new laws require lenders to make a good-faith assessment of a borrower’s capacity to pay back their loan over time.
[p] It’s a longer-term view that goes beyond immediate income, debt and credit rating.
[p] These new Federal laws- supervised by the CFPB - require lenders to ask more questions –
[p] about income, assets, employment, credit history, and monthly expenses –
[p] as they relate to the proposed loan.
[p] For example, a lender offering a mortgage with a low initial rate must try to assess how a borrower will handle the later, higher rate as well.
[p] If you’re applying to borrow ask whether the program you’re considering is a Qualified Mortgage
[p] Ability-to-repay rules are built in to loans that meet Qualified Mortgage guidelines.
