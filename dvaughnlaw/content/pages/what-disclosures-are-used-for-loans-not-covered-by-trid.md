[h1] What Disclosures Are Used For Loans Not Covered By TRID?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/eioaubloyf
[p] Creditors must continue to use the Good Faith Estimate, Truth-In-Lending Disclosure and the HUD-1 form for reverse mortgages, HELOCs, mobile home or other non-attached dwelling loans and others NOT covered by TRID.
[p] Housing assistance loans for low- and moderate-income consumers are partially exempt from TRID disclosures, and have specific rules.
[p] Creditors are not required to provide Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure forms and related booklets and statements for these loans.
