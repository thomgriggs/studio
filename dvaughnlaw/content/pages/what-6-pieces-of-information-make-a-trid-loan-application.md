[h1] What 6 Pieces of Information Make A TRID Loan Application?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/a2tdvx79vj
[p] Submitting these 6 pieces of information:
[ul]
- Name
- Income
- Social Security Number
- Property Address
- Estimated Value of Property
- Mortgage Loan Amount sought
[p] constitutes a valid loan application under the TRID rule.
[p] You may apply and submit these in writing OR in oral form; a live conversation, or a phone call, backed by a written record of the conversation is a legitimate application.
[p] Once these 6 pieces of information are submitted a creditor MUST supply a Loan Estimate for approved loans within 3 business days.
