[h1] What Is A Certificate of Eligibility, or COE?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/mxxjw1fg33
[p] What Is A Certificate of Eligibility, or COE?
[p] The COE is the key document that verifies to lenders that someone is eligible for a VA-backed loan.
[p] Servicemembers, Veterans and National Guard and Reserve members may apply online or through their lender; most lenders have access to the system and can verify eligibility IF the VA has records on file.
[p] The VA also maintains a hotline for assistance.
[p] Surviving Spouses can use VA Form 26-1817 to request determination of their eligibility for VA Loan Guarantees.
[p] Your lender may be able to assist with processing or contact the VA for information this video did not address.
