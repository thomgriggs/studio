[h1] Can Creditors Collect Information Beyond The 6 Required Pieces?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/ot81c1pdm4
[p] In addition to the required pieces:
[ul]
- Name
- Income
- Social Security Number
- Property Address
- Estimated Property Value
- Mortgage Amount Sought
[p] a creditor may collect whatever additional information they deem necessary.
[p] However, as soon as you have provided the 6 required pieces, the creditor has 3 business days to provide a Loan Estimate for approved loans.
