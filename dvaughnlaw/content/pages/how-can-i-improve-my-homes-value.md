[h1] How Can I Improve My Home’s Value?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/tis06sm9zm
[p] Buyers generally seek the least expensive home in the best neighborhood they can handle. Like the guy in the video says, you want to present a home that fits in the neighborhood but doesn’t stand out too much.
[p] For example if neighbors are all 4 bedrooms, 3 baths and 3000 square feet additions that make your home 5, 4, and 4000 will make yours harder to sell.
[p] Improvements should make it show well and fit well in the neighborhood. Last-minute capital investments in large structural changes aren’t likely to pay off.
[p] But cosmetic upgrades like paint and landscaping help a home “show” better and often do pay off.
[p] Of course, all systems and appliances should work to get a top price. To make your home competitive and attract buyers and bids work with a professional real estate agent and start early.
