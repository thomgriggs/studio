[h1] How Do Lenders Decide The Maximum Loan Amount That Buyers Can Afford?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/q8lt0c8yx7
[p] As you’ll see in the video, the lenders consider your debt-to-income ratio, which is a comparison of your gross (pre-tax) income to housing and non-housing expenses.
[p] Non-housing expenses include such long-term debts as car or student loan payments, alimony, or child support.
[p] According to the FHA, monthly mortgage payments should be no more than 29% of gross income, while the mortgage payment, combined with non-housing expenses, should total no more than 41% of income. Lenders also consider cash available for down payment and closing costs credit history and the rest of your financial picture when determining your maximum loan amount.
