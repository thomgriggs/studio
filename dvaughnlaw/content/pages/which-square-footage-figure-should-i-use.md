[h1] Which Square Footage Figure Should I Use?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/ilqlwpe9ac
[p] Home size is one of the key figures used in comparisons.
[p] But you may have different measurements to choose from, as you’ll learn in this video, including builder, appraiser, tax records and possibly owner records.
[p] Which one is right, and which one is best?
[p] The official figure is the one in tax records – typically, the county.
[p] Any other figure must be documented by a builder’s floor plan
[p] an appraisal or an official floor plan, prepared by a company for a fee.
[p] If your house has been remodeled and you’re planning to sell
[p] you may want to confirm that the official record matches your actual house – and update if required.
[p] Most lenders will require an appraisal which will verify the figures you used. So be accurate and keep records to make the most of your sale.
