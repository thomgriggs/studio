[h1] How Should I Prepare For Internet Showing?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/3t6iif3edk
[p] Today, your first “showing” will be on the Internet – you’re watching this on the Internet, right?
[p] Your price, listing description and PHOTOS determine whether someone will visit in person. Consider professional staging advice or help.
[p] Prep for photos and video just as carefully as real visits.
[p] Ask your realtor if they use a professional photographer
[p] If they do look at prior photos and pick someone who understands the job.
[p] Photos should make the most of your home’s features and give prospective buyers an emotional connection that invites them to visit in person.
[p] Help them envision their lifestyle in the house not just the counters and walls.
[p] If your realtor recommends video, just as with photography stage it carefully and hire a professional it will pay off.
[p] And look over your listing when it goes live on a computer AND a mobile device to make sure it’s accurate, pleasant and compels people to show up.
[p] Remember – your first showing these days will be on a screen.
