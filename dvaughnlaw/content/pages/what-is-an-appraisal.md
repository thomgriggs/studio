[h1] What Is An Appraisal?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/pdmj9rfxp9
[p] Every house is unique; appraisers are trained and licensed for expertise in putting a value on properties.
[p] Appraisers don’t work for the buyer or the seller; their primary mission is actually to protect the lender who’s risking money against the home’s value.
[p] Appraisers have to weigh factors about the property and location – including size, condition and comparable properties – to appraise its current value.
[p] They know how to focus on conditions that affect value; dishes in the sink don’t; damage and neglect do.
[p] Appraisals lower than the proposed purchase price can affect transaction details. The seller might have to lower the price
[p] or the buyer might have to increase down payment or fund additional escrow.
[p] Appraisal seems a lot like inspection, but they’re not the same.
[p] You can think of it this way:
[p] Appraisers report on value to the lender
[p] Inspectors report on condition of the house and major components to the buyer.
[p] So – expect both appraisal & inspection in your transaction.
