[h1] How Should I Prepare The Outside For An Open House?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/tw328qz4e6
[p] Professional “staging” may include the exterior, but if you’re doing it all yourself, try the five things outlined in this video.
[p] **1 – Landscape & lawn.**
[p] That’s the first impression; make it a good one.
[p] Mow, prune, edge and get rid of junk!
[p] **2 – Paint And Clean!**
[p] You don’t have to do the whole house, but the front door and lintels should either be painted or cleaned.
[p] **3 – Leaks & Repairs**
[p] Small visible problems can become large mental objections and change how someone feels about your house.
[p] Fix ‘em beforehand.
[p] **4 – Pets**
[p] Some people have allergies and concerns.
[p] Time for Fido to visit a friend.
[p] You weren’t including him with the house anyway.
[p] **5 – Get Fresh Eyes**
[p] Have your realtor or a friend who’s willing to be candid tell you what you missed. Or pay a staging professional for a report.
[p] We don’t really see familiar things well – so let them be your
[p] ‘test buyer’ so you can present the best first impression to the real ones.
