[h1] How Can The FHA Assist Me In Buying A Home?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/es4hquysti
[p] Remember these points from the video:the FHA works to make homeownership a possibility for more Americans. With the FHA, you don’t need perfect credit or a high-paying job to qualify for a loan. The FHA also makes loans more accessible by requiring smaller down payments than conventional loans. In fact, an FHA down payment could be as little as a few months rent. And your monthly payments may not be much more than rent.
