[h1] What Is The Best Way To Compare Loan Terms Between Lenders?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/ma26qc9ne7
[p] Watch this video and take a few notes!
[p] First, devise a checklist for the information from each lending institution. You should include:
[ul]
- the company’s name and basic information
- the type of mortgage
- minimum down payment required
- interest rate and points
- closing costs
- loan processing time
- whether prepayment is allowed
[p] Speak with companies by phone or in person. Be sure to call every lender on the list the same day as interest rates can fluctuate daily.
[p] In addition to doing your own research your real estate agent may have access to a database of lender and mortgage options or suggest a variety of different lender options.
