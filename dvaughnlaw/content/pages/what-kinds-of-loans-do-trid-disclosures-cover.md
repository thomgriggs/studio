[h1] What Kinds Of Loans Do TRID Disclosures Cover?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/kk8hv5upf7
[p] TRID rules apply to MOST consumer credit transactions secured by real property. These include mortgages, refinancing, construction-only loans closed-end home-equity loans, and loans secured by vacant land or by 25 or more acres.
[p] The rule does NOT apply to Home Equity Line of Credit transactions reverse mortgages mortgages secured by a mobile home or other dwelling that is not attached to real property.
[p] Also, TRID rules do NOT apply to loans made by a person or business that makes 5 or fewer mortgages in a calendar year.
