[h1] What Types Of Closing Costs Are Associated With FHA-Insured Loans?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/xdgdwr21ds
[p] While this video simplifies things to help you remember, except for the addition of an FHA mortgage insurance premium, FHA closing costs are similar to those of a conventional loan.
[p] As of 2013, the FHA requires a single, upfront mortgage insurance premium equal to 2.25% of the mortgage to be paid at closing (or 1.75% if you complete the HELP program).
[p] This initial premium may be partially refunded if the loan is paid in full during the first seven years of the loan term.
[p] After closing, you will then be responsible for an annual premium – paid monthly – if your mortgage is over 15 years or if you have a 15-year loan with an LTV greater than 90%.
