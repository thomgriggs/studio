[h1] What Is A Mortgage?
[embed wistia] https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/ebt6j7h3uj
[p] The original phrase “mort gage” translates as “death pledge”! But as this video explains, a mortgage is a loan obtained to purchase real estate. The “mortgage” itself is a lien – a legal claim on the home or property that secures the promise to pay the debt.
[p] All mortgages have two features in common: principal and interest.
[p] The principal is the amount you are borrowing which is “secured” by the lender’s claim on the property.
[p] The interest, usually stated as the percentage rate is the additional amount paid for borrowing. Mortgage interest is ‘compounded’ – interest on interest, over time.
