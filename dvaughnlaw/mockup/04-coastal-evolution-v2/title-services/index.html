About Vaughn Law

Vaughn Law is a boutique law firm that provides full-service title and real estate closing services in the state of Florida.

A closing can be overwhelming, unfamiliar and exciting for everyone involved, so we give each closing personal attention. Our goal is to make it as smooth and as easy as possible for all parties.

Whether you are a real estate developer or buying or selling your first home, we can handle your closing. We are licensed to practice throughout Florida, not only in the Tampa Bay area.

Our team pays attention to the details, and we keep developing resources and technology to help you.
