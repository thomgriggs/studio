What estate planning is

Estate planning is arranging, during your lifetime, what happens to your property. It aims to remove or reduce the uncertainties around it, and to cut taxes and other expenses where it can.

The goal of an estate plan comes from your own goals. It can be as simple or as complex as your needs call for.

There are five documents to consider, whatever your age, health or wealth:

  • Durable Power of Attorney
  • Advanced Medical Directive
  • Last Will and Testament (Will)
  • Trust
  • Letter of Instruction

Having a will does not avoid probate. If you want to avoid probate, you need a trust-based estate plan.
