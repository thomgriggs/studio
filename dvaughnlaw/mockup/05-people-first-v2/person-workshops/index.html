Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this workshop really free?

Yes — completely free. No cost to attend, no obligation to purchase anything, and no pressure. Vaughn Law hosts these workshops because we genuinely care about protecting families in our community.

Who is this workshop for?

Anyone who has family, owns anything, or wants to make sure their wishes are honored. Whether you’re 30 or 70, a first-time planner or someone with an outdated will — this workshop was designed for you.

How long is the workshop?

Approximately two hours. We start on time, end on time, and reserve time at the end for questions and one-on-one conversation with our attorney(s).

Should I bring my spouse or partner?

Absolutely — we encourage it. Estate planning is a family decision, and couples who attend together get much more out of the experience. Just note how many guests are coming when you register.

What is the Legacy Design Meeting?

A private, one-hour session with one of our estate planning attorneys where they design your personal estate plan — built specifically for your family situation. Available to workshop attendees at $200 (regularly $350), with your full $200 credited toward your completed estate plan.

Who is this workshop for?

Anyone who has family, owns anything, or wants to make sure their wishes are honored. Whether you’re 30 or 70, a first-time planner or someone with an outdated will — this workshop was designed for you.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just yourself and your questions. If you have an existing will or trust, you’re welcome to bring it — but it’s not required to attend or benefit from the workshop.

What if I can't make any of the listed dates?

Use the waitlist link below the event listings and we’ll contact you as soon as a new workshop is available. We add new dates every month across St. Petersburg and Tampa.
