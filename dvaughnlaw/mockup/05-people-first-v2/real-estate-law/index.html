About Vaughn Law

Vaughn Law is a boutique law firm that provides practical real estate legal solutions for litigation and transactional needs.

We take a more personal, service-oriented approach. We tailor our representation to each client, and the client is always our first priority.

This can be an overwhelming, unfamiliar and stressful time. We help you understand the legal system, keep you informed throughout, and give honest and straightforward opinions on all your options, so you can make fully informed decisions.

Every situation is unique. We have handled many legal cases throughout Florida.
