## Conflicts to resolve before implementation

Proposal has an earlier 45-page estimate, but its detailed count is about 24 pages plus 65 articles. Treat the detailed sitemap as the basis for implementation.
The live homepage includes a Lawmatics form although the older inventory says no form. The future simplified intake remains the design target; confirm routing/integration.
Some draft legal explanations and marketing claims need attorney review; this concept uses restrained general descriptions instead of reproducing categorical guarantees.
