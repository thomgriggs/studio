# Concept 02 — Editorial

## Idea
Magazine-first. Danielle's writing leads the home page: masthead headline, a feature article with photo, three more, then the practice. Paper white, ink black, the blue only as link color and one pale panel. Big condensed grotesk headlines (Archivo wdth 87.5) over a readable serif body (Source Serif 4). Two heavy rules structure the page; no cards. The firm reads as a publisher with a practice attached.
