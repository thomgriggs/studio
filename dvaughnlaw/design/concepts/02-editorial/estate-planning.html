Wills and trusts

A will says who gets what and who's in charge. A trust can do that without a court. Which one you need depends on what you own, who you're leaving it to, and whether you'd rather spend a little now or have your family spend more later. We'll tell you plainly which fits.

Wills and trusts

Probate

Probate is the court process for passing on what someone owned. In Florida it can be short or long depending on what was planned ahead of time. We handle formal, summary and ancillary probate for families here and for out-of-state owners of Florida property.

Powers of attorney and health care documents

The documents that matter while you're alive: who can sign for you, who can speak to your doctors, and what you'd want. Every adult should have these; most don't.

Powers of attorney

Your home is part of the plan

For most families on the beach the house is the biggest thing they own. A Lady Bird deed or a deed into a trust can keep it out of probate entirely — and because we're a real estate firm, we draft and record that deed ourselves. Nothing gets handed off.
