# Design direction

Brief in one line: a small, expert, local firm with a founder who writes.
Gulf Coast light, modern edge, the writing as the visual. Not navy-and-gold lawyer.

## Sources
- The office: St. Pete Beach, light-blue building, modern sign. Site should feel like walking up to that door.
- Danielle's voice: "Vaughn Voyage," frequent posts, YouTube. Editorial, personal, direct.
- The audience: anxious about cost and being talked down to. Treat them as capable.

## Principles
- One typeface pairing, large.
- Real photos only. Retire the stock.
- Five nav items + Contact button. Nav never changes.
- Each service page is one screen: what, how it starts, cost shape, one button.
- Articles as a magazine, by topic, video beside the post.
- Coastal palette: building blue as brand, sand, white, dark ink, one accent. No gold.
- Trust at the top: board cert (confirm), years on the beach, reviews, the beach towns named.

## References
- alhlaw.com — Thom's pick. Tone; guides as a section.
- lulich.com — structure; consult form w/ practice picker; closing + cost guides.
- alperlaw.com — three-step process block.
- Boutique coastal hotel sites — palette, restraint, place as brand.
- Editorial/newsletter sites — how articles should feel.

Don't borrow from law-firm "best of" lists built on sticky phone numbers.
